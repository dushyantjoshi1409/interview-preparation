# Company Interview Checklist

Copy this checklist into a new `companies/<company-slug>/` directory.

## Intake

- [ ] Company name
- [ ] Role title
- [ ] Job-description URL and access date
- [ ] Interview date, time, time zone, duration, and format
- [ ] Resume version submitted for the role
- [ ] Recruiter or interviewer role, without publishing private contact details

## Research

- [ ] Official company overview, products, values, and recent relevant initiatives
- [ ] Official technical, security, compliance, and Responsible AI material
- [ ] Candidate-reported interview experiences, labeled anecdotal
- [ ] Likely 30-minute agenda
- [ ] Role-specific technical and coding themes

## Resume alignment

- [ ] 60-90 second introduction
- [ ] Why this company?
- [ ] Why this role?
- [ ] Resume-to-job match map
- [ ] Four truthful STAR stories
- [ ] Measurement details for every resume metric
- [ ] Honest scripts for skill or experience gaps

## Technical preparation

- [ ] Architecture and system-design questions
- [ ] Programming-language questions
- [ ] API and database questions
- [ ] Cloud, security, monitoring, and testing questions
- [ ] Coding exercises with solutions and complexity
- [ ] Real-world analogies in easy English

## Practice

- [ ] Timed mock interview
- [ ] Record and review the introduction
- [ ] Practice concise follow-up answers
- [ ] Select two interviewer questions
- [ ] Complete the final equipment and environment check

## After the interview

- [ ] Record questions asked
- [ ] Record strong and weak answers
- [ ] Note promised follow-ups
- [ ] Add lessons for the next company
