# Job Description Summary

## Company

Textron Specialized Vehicles manufactures golf cars, utility and personal transportation vehicles, turf-care equipment, and ground support equipment. Its products are used in golf courses, factories, airports, planned communities, theme parks, and hunting environments.

## Role mission

Build secure, scalable, user-facing AI applications that turn data-science models and governed enterprise data into reliable conversational and workflow-based products.

## Main responsibilities

- Productionize AI models, prompts, model outputs, and confidence logic.
- Design conversational flows, orchestration, escalation rules, confidence thresholds, and human handoffs.
- Build and maintain backend and frontend application components.
- Integrate applications with approved systems such as SAP, Salesforce, and governed knowledge repositories.
- Enforce authentication, authorization, data governance, security, compliance, and Responsible AI requirements.
- Add logging, telemetry, monitoring, feedback loops, and business-performance measurements.
- Collaborate with data science, IT, platform, security, and business teams.

## Requested background

- Bachelor's degree in computer science, information systems, analytics, statistics, or a related field.
- At least one year of relevant experience.
- Production software-development experience, preferably in an enterprise environment.
- Python preferred, with C#, ASP.NET, Java, or equivalent languages also relevant.
- Web applications, APIs, services, modern frontend technologies, SQL, and relational data concepts.
- Cloud development and AI platforms, with Azure preferred.
- Git-based collaboration, testing, documentation, and code-review practices.
- Familiarity with CRM, ERP, service platforms, and enterprise data integration.
